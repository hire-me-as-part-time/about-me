THE EARTH

Our planet, the third rock from the Sun, is a dynamic sphere of life and wonder. Covered in vast oceans, continents, and teeming with biodiversity, Earth is the only known world to harbor life. Rotating on its axis once every 24 hours, it orbits the Sun every 365.25 days, creating the rhythms of day, night, and seasons that define existence.

Diameter: 12,742 km

Distance from Sun: 149.6 million km (1 AU)

Orbital Period: 365.25 days
